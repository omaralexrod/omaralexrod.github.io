# Omar Rodriguez Arellano — Data Science Portfolio

Live site: [omaralexrod.github.io](https://omaralexrod.github.io)

Data science student — projects in predictive modeling, statistics, and data visualization. Each project below has its own repo with a full write-up, code, and results.

## Projects

- [Olympic Participation Trends](https://github.com/omaralexrod/olympic-participation-trends) - analysis of 120+ years of Olympic athlete data, covering gender participation, top medal-winning countries, and top medal-awarding sports.
- [Airbnb Revenue Prediction - Austin, TX](https://github.com/omaralexrod/airbnb-revenue-prediction) - Random Forest model predicting monthly Airbnb revenue from listing features (R² of 0.876).
- [NFL Draft Round vs. Fantasy Performance](https://github.com/omaralexrod/nfl-draft-round-fantasy) - statistical test of whether first-round draft picks outperform later rounds.
- [U.S. Retail Sales Forecasting](https://github.com/omaralexrod/retail-sales-forecasting) - SARIMA time series forecasting of U.S. monthly retail sales.
- [Movie Recommender System](https://github.com/omaralexrod/movie-recommender) - item-based collaborative filtering recommender using cosine similarity.
- [NFL Fantasy Points Prediction](https://github.com/omaralexrod/nfl-fantasy-points-prediction) - predicting next-game fantasy points across Linear, Ridge, Lasso, and Random Forest models.
- [Netflix Global Content Strategy](https://github.com/omaralexrod/netflix-content-strategy) - data-driven presentation on Netflix's non-English content strategy.
- [White House Visitor Log Analysis](https://github.com/omaralexrod/white-house-visitor-logs) - exploratory analysis of public White House visitor logs.
- [EV Market Analysis](https://github.com/omaralexrod/ev-market-analysis) - integrating three separate EV data sources into a SQLite database to analyze sales trends, pricing, and charging infrastructure.
- [NFL Total Points Prediction](https://github.com/omaralexrod/nfl-total-points-prediction) - full ML pipeline predicting NFL game totals from team EPA trends, benchmarked against the Vegas line.
